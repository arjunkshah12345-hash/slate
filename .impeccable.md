## Design Context

### Users
Founders, marketers, and editors pairing with ChatGPT in the in-app browser. They cut a launch on a shared timeline.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, native, considered. Three words: **Shotbase-calm**. A Mac capture workspace that happens to edit time.

### Aesthetic Direction
Reference: [shotbase.com](https://shotbase.com) / Shotbase app — dark library, white detail card, electric blue actions, pill filters, dotted canvas, framed preview, sidebar shot list.
Not: warm editorial paper, Fraunces luxury, tungsten film-set, neon AI, Inter-on-slate templates.

Type: system UI / SF stack for chrome. Mono only for timecode. Plates may keep their own display type.

### Design Principles
1. Dark workspace + one light inspector. That contrast is the product.
2. Shots live in a TOC, not only a filmstrip.
3. The cut sits in a framed canvas on a dotted field.
4. Blue means act. Pins mean stop.
5. Agent presence is a chip, not a chatbot panel.
