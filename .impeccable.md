## Design Context

### Users
Founders and editors pairing with ChatGPT in a browser. They cut a short launch on shared stills.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, expensive, photographic. Three words: **void, still, clap**.

### Aesthetic Direction
Linear (void, muted nav, white act), Raycast (island chrome, one line of type), Cap (the product is a framed still). Shotbase only for the white sheet of notes.

Not: dotted fields, polaroid collages, blue pill spam, overlay modals, system-font SaaS.

Type: Geist. One family, a few sizes. Mono only for timecode.

### Design Principles
1. Remove chrome until the still is the page.
2. The only loud thing is the photograph.
3. Act is white, not blue. Blue is a hold fill only.
4. Island nav. Never a glued bar.
5. Inspector is a quiet sheet, not a chatbot.
